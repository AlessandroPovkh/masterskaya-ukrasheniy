# Storefront override

The generated MASTER is advisory research, not brand authority. Its Liquid Glass, pink/violet palette and Amatic/Cabin typography are rejected because they conflict with the approved v2.0 brand system.

Storefront authority:

- organic editorial commerce, warm matte paper texture, no glass/blur/chromatic effects;
- Prata 400 display, Manrope 400/500/600 interface, Cormorant Garamond Medium Italic only for short accents;
- `#F4EEE7`, `#EDE288`, `#E5D5E1`, `#D5D08C` surfaces and `#771441` text/actions;
- official Organic Stone master SVG only;
- 150–250 ms transform/opacity interactions and reduced-motion fallback;
- clear availability, price and purchase/inquiry paths take precedence over atmosphere.
